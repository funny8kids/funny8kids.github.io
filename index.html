Chapter III · Keeper's notes

How this garden is tended

The Garden Keeper stands at the end of the runway, beside the desk your letters are folded on. She is drawn by the same ink as this sentence — the nib writes her from nothing, feet first — and the lamp beside her is this corner's only key light.

  • One law, not fifty effects

    Ink is the garden. The cursor is a pen, the type is extruded onto stems, the letter folds into a flower, and a memory you leave here is still growing when you come back.

  • Modelled, not downloaded

    Every violet, every stalk and every letterform is generated in Blender and compressed for the wire. No store-bought asset touches this soil.

  • Fast on a small phone

    Instanced, lazy-loaded, and measured: the frame budget is a design constraint, not an afterthought.
